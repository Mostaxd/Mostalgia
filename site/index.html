Episode 1 · 第一話 録音

Press record. Or don't. It starts with your call.

Mori

Two tracks, always

Mostalgia records your microphone and the other side of the call (desktop audio) as separate tracks, so every line knows if it came from Me or Others. Readable device names, live level meters, and a nudge when your mic has gone suspiciously quiet.

Auto-start with calls

Mostalgia notices when a call app grabs the mic and offers to record (or just starts), then stops when the call ends.

  • Zoom
  • Teams
  • Meet
  • Discord
  • Slack
  • Signal
  • Element
  • Jitsi
  • Webex
  • Telegram
  • WhatsApp
  • any call in your browser

Stay in the moment

  • Mark a moment as a bookmark, pause when you need to.
  • Switch the mic or desktop audio on and off mid-recording; the transcript shows where.
  • Tray icon and a global shortcut: Super + Shift + R.
  • After Stop, the meeting appears straight away with real progress while it's processed.

Crash-safe recording

Tracks are flushed and synced to disk every 2 seconds, from their own threads, and never wait for transcription. If the app or the laptop dies mid-meeting, Mostalgia repairs the tracks at the next launch and runs the normal final pass, so you lose seconds, not the meeting.

Bring the meetings you already have

Import recordings: any audio or video file becomes a meeting, with speakers, summary and notes. Or create a meeting from text: paste or open a Teams, Zoom or Meet export, WebVTT/SRT subtitles or plain notes.

Episode 2 · 第二話 文字起こし

Every word, as it's spoken. Whisper turbo on your own GPU.

Hoshi

Whisper, locally, fast

Whisper large-v3-turbo through whisper.cpp on Vulkan (NVIDIA, AMD and Intel, no extra toolkits), CUDA, or the CPU. Around 30× faster than real time on a laptop GPU, multilingual, and nothing leaves your machine.

Words in about a second

Provisional text appears after ~1 s while someone is still talking, then is replaced by the clean line when they pause.

00:15OthersWe're about ten percent under plan right now.
00:27Me

Clean by design

  • Silero VAD decides what's speech, so silence never turns into hallucinated lines.
  • Optional noise suppression (RNNoise) for fans and keyboard clicks; raw tracks stay untouched.
  • After you stop, a final pass re-transcribes the full tracks for the archive.

Any language

Mostalgia auto-detects the language or lets you pin one. Right-to-left transcripts like Arabic render properly, and the summary is written in the meeting's own language.

Floating live captions

An optional always-on-top caption window shows the latest lines and who spoke, above Teams or Zoom, without stealing keyboard focus. Flag a line to bookmark it; place, size and opacity are remembered.

Live · 12:41 ◐

Anna Could you send a new forecast by Friday?

Me Sure, I just need the numbers from sales

Local, Hybrid or Cloud, and no silent fallbacks

Use Whisper on your GPU, a self-hosted Whisper server, or a cloud service (OpenAI, Groq) for machines without a GPU, chosen separately for live text and the final pass, with the cost per hour shown. If a provider fails you see which one and why, the audio stays on disk, and Transcribe again lets you pick another engine. Nothing switches behind your back.

Episode 3 · 第三話 話者

Who said it? Named once, remembered next time.

Jin

Me and Others, for free

Because your mic and the call are recorded separately, every line is already split into what you said and what the others said. No model needed.

Speaker diarization

Optional offline diarization (sherpa-onnx with pyannote segmentation and WeSpeaker embeddings) splits the other side into Speaker 1, Speaker 2 and so on.

Rename once, known forever

Rename a speaker and every line follows. The voice is remembered: Speaker 2 → Felix today is labelled Felix automatically in the next meeting. Only a voice print is kept, never audio, and you can manage or forget known voices in Settings → Speakers.

Decision log

An optional Decisions page lists every decision across all your meetings, newest first, each tied to the line where it was said and who said it. Search and filter by meeting, person and date, edit entries, or mark one as superseded by a later decision.

Episode 5 · 第五話 質問

Ask your meetings. They remember everything.

Tsumugi

Answers with sources you can play

Ask in plain words. Answers come with clickable sources that open the meeting at that line and play the audio.

What did Anna promise last week?
A new forecast by Friday 1, once she has the numbers from sales 2.

Hybrid search, all local

BM25 keywords plus multilingual e5 embeddings, fused with reciprocal rank fusion, in about 25 ms over your whole library. Cloud models only see the few passages they look up. Local models work too: one without tool calling simply gets the search results up front.

Honest when nobody said it

Nobody said that, at least not while Mostalgia was listening.

Ask live, mid-meeting

Click your companion and ask about the live meeting (“What did we decide so far?”), the meeting that's open, or all meetings. Answers stream in with numbered sources that jump to the moment; every recording keeps its own chat.

Stuck? A suggested answer, with proof

When someone asks you a question mid-call, your companion quietly checks your past meetings and documents and slides in a suggested answer, with the sources it's based on, so you can answer with confidence. If nothing backs an answer up, it stays silent instead of guessing.

Collections, switched on or off

Group documents into collections and drag files straight onto one. Switch a whole collection, or a single document, on or off for answers in Ask, the chat and live suggestions.

Ask one collection, or one document

Meet the cast. Pick who takes your notes.

モリ

The note-taker

Mori

How notes appear

📌

Live notes

Every 90 seconds or so (or as it happens) your companion pins up decisions, action items with owners, open questions, numbers and key points. Pick Default or Modern detailed, add a focus hint, pin what matters. Everything is saved in the meeting's Notes tab.

🪟

On your desktop

Drag them out of the window and they float above everything, your call app included, anywhere across your monitors. Hide them into a slim bar with record, pause and mic controls.

🔁

Regenerate after the meeting

Not happy with the notes? Regenerate them with a new focus or style, undo, or restore earlier ones.

💬

Honest about what they do

The bubble shows whether a local or cloud model is answering. No model set up? They say so. A provider fails? You get a worried face and the reason, never a pretend answer.

😴

Alive, but calm

They idle, listen, write, think, talk, doze off when you pause, and look worried when something breaks. Small next to the transcript, never on top of it.

🎛️

Entirely optional

Off by default. Switch on in Settings → Companion, choose placement (app window or desktop), size and how often notes are taken.

Episode 8 · 第八話 秘密

Your meetings stay yours. You always see where data goes.

Jin

Local, Hybrid or Cloud

Local

Whisper on your GPU, a local model for summaries and Ask.

Leaves your laptop: nothing

Hybrid · default

Whisper on your GPU, a cloud provider for summaries and Ask.

Leaves: meeting text for summaries, only the few passages Ask looks up

Cloud

A cloud speech-to-text service, any AI provider.

Leaves: audio to the transcription provider, text as above

Where everything lives

  • Recordings always stay on your disk, whatever the mode.
  • The search index for Ask (BM25 + embeddings) is always local.
  • API keys live in the GNOME keyring and go only to their own provider.

Auto cleanup

Delete meeting audio after 7, 14, 30 or 90 days (or any number), and keep everything else: transcript, summary, notes. It asks the first time, never touches a meeting that's open or pinned with Keep audio, and logs what it removed.

Forget a meeting, completely

Delete a meeting and it's removed from the library, the search index and, if you tick the box, the disk.

Source and full guide on GitHub

# 3. App grid entry and the Super+Shift+R shortcut
./scripts/install-desktop.sh
./scripts/install-gnome-shortcut.sh

# 4. Connect an AI for summaries and Ask
mostalgia connections add ollama
